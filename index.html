Privacy Policy

Orrion AI — Last updated: May 26, 2026

1. Overview

This privacy policy describes how Orrion AI ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our automation tools and applications, including tools that publish content to social media platforms on behalf of users.

2. Information We Collect

Our applications collect only the minimum data required to function:

  • OAuth tokens: Access tokens issued by third-party platforms (Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit) when you authorise our app. These are used solely to post content on your behalf.
  • Content metadata: Titles, descriptions, and URLs of content being published, as provided by you.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, payment details, or browsing history.

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate with third-party platforms using your authorised credentials
  • Publish content to platforms on your behalf as instructed
  • Maintain session continuity for automated workflows

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the core automation function.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the platforms you have explicitly authorised (Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit) as part of the publishing process.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are stored only as long as necessary to maintain your active workflow. You may revoke access at any time by disconnecting the app from your platform account settings.

6. Third-Party Platforms

Our tools interact with third-party services including Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Reddit. Your use of those platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies. We are not responsible for their data practices.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data. OAuth tokens are stored securely and are not exposed publicly.

8. Your Rights

You may revoke this application's access to any platform at any time through that platform's app settings. This will immediately stop any automated publishing.
